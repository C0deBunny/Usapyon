# Usapyon — Music Direction

> The body of this document is the prompt used when generating Usapyon music.

Cozy kawaii virtual-pet game background music. Soft music-box melody, gentle glockenspiel, warm muted piano, subtle plucked strings, tiny soft percussion, occasional playful woodwind accents. Peaceful, innocent, comforting and slightly dreamy. Japanese cozy-game atmosphere. Simple memorable melody, very light arrangement, no dramatic changes, no vocals, no singing. Designed to loop unobtrusively while caring for a cute bunny pet. Around 80–95 BPM.
