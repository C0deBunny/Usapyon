# Usapyon — Art Direction

> The body of this document is the prompt used when generating Usapyon art assets.

Create this asset in the established Usapyon visual style: a cute, polished 2D kawaii sticker-style mobile game aesthetic.

VISUAL LANGUAGE:

- Cute Japanese-inspired kawaii character design.
- Clean, rounded, soft shapes with an adorable toy/sticker-like appearance.
- Simple, highly readable silhouettes designed to remain clear on a small phone screen.
- Friendly, cozy, wholesome and playful mood.
- Modern mobile-game quality, not retro or pixel art.
- Vector-like raster artwork with smooth, clean edges.
- Avoid realism and excessive detail.

LINEWORK:

- Strong, smooth outer outlines.
- Outlines should be dark navy / blue-black rather than pure black.
- Consistent rounded line weight.
- Interior details use slightly thinner lines where appropriate.
- No sketchy, rough, painterly or textured linework.

COLOR:

- Soft pastel color palette.
- Colors should feel cheerful, warm and slightly muted rather than highly saturated.
- Use harmonious pastel pinks, blues, creams, greens, yellows, lavenders and warm neutrals.
- Avoid muddy colors, harsh contrast and neon colors unless specifically requested.
- Dark outlines provide most of the visual contrast.

SHADING:

- Mostly flat-color illustration.
- Very minimal soft cel shading where useful for volume.
- At most one simple shadow tone and occasional small highlight.
- No realistic lighting, complex gradients, painterly rendering or detailed texture.
- The result should remain easy to reproduce consistently across many game assets.

CHARACTER DESIGN:

- Characters use cute chibi proportions.
- Large head relative to the body.
- Small, rounded body and limbs.
- Soft curves and slightly squishy proportions.
- Facial features are simple but extremely expressive.
- Large dark glossy oval eyes with small clean highlights.
- Tiny simple mouth and nose.
- Expressions must remain readable at small sizes.
- Bunny ears are an important part of emotional expression and silhouette.

CONSISTENCY:

- Prioritize consistency with existing Usapyon assets over adding new visual details.
- Do not redesign established characters when creating new poses, expressions, clothing or accessories.
- Preserve established proportions, face placement, eye design, outline style and palette.
- Clothing and accessories should look like modular additions to the existing character rather than changing the underlying character.
- Keep designs simple enough that assets can be layered, animated and reused in a 2D mobile game.

PRESENTATION:

- Clean game-ready asset.
- No text unless explicitly requested.
- No watermark.
- No photorealism.
- No 3D render appearance.
- No pixel art.
- No anime-style detailed rendering.
- No painterly brushwork.
- No complex textures.
